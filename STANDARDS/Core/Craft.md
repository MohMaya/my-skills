# Core/Craft.md

Reading list behind the kernel's ground rules and code gate. No rules of its own -- the operative rules live in `Core/Code.md` and `Core/Planning.md`. Load only when a one-liner needs its source argument.

Expertise and judgment:
- Ericsson, *Deliberate Practice* -- designed hours, not hours.
- Dreyfus & Dreyfus, *Mind Over Machine* -- novice→expert; rule-following is a novice signature.
- Kahneman & Klein, 2009 -- intuition is trustworthy only in high-validity, fast-feedback domains.
- Chase & Simon, 1973; Klein, *Sources of Power* -- expertise is compressed perception; it fails out of distribution.
- Soloway & Ehrlich, 1984; Adelson, 1984 -- experts read more, write less, organize by deep plans.

Taste and subtraction:
- Graham, "Taste for Makers" -- good design is simple and solves the right problem.
- Rubin, *The Creative Act*; Tufte, *Visual Display* -- serve the work; every element earns its place.
- Saint-Exupéry; Klotz, *Subtract*; Adams et al., *Nature* 2021 -- humans overlook subtractive fixes under load.
- Strunk & White; Pascal; Hemingway's iceberg -- brevity is the expensive output, and only compression backed by knowledge holds.

Engineering:
- Dijkstra -- simplicity is a prerequisite for reliability; count lines spent.
- Kernighan -- debugging is twice as hard as writing.
- Ousterhout, *A Philosophy of Software Design* -- deep modules, small interfaces, strategic programming.
- Fowler/Beck; Metz -- speculative generality; duplication is cheaper than the wrong abstraction.

Focus:
- Newport, *Deep Work*; Leroy, 2009 -- attention residue; one role per task.
- de Bono -- separate generation from evaluation.
- shokunin; Jiro Ono -- standards hold when no one is watching.
