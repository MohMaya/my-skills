## Three-noun evidence close (Daily Newspaper Briefing, 2026-09-02, Knight slot)

Source/Author: own newspaper draft, Knight Capital section → the section ends
"Forty-five minutes, four million orders, ninety-seven unread emails" — three
noun phrases, each an already-cited fact, no new claim. The compression does
the arguing: the reader re-weighs the evidence the piece already established.
→ Apply at the end of an argument whose evidence is fully on the table; the
third item should be the strangest. → Do not apply in openings or more than
once per piece; three of these in one article read as a tic.
