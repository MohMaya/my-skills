# Grilling

Establish the objective, constraints, material assumptions, failure modes, and verification before committing to a consequential choice.

Read available evidence first. Research facts independently; ask the user for preferences, missing access, or decisions only they can make.

Ask one to three questions per round. Prioritize answers that change scope, architecture, risk, or success criteria. Give a recommendation and its tradeoff. Keep dependent questions for later rounds.

Use the answers to revise the plan. State reasonable assumptions for reversible details and continue independent work while a question remains open. Delegate substantial, independent research when the runtime supports it.

Stop when the remaining uncertainty permits the authorized work to proceed. Summarize the decision and material risks. A request to stress-test alone ends with that assessment. Continue implementation when it is already authorized. Obtain approval for consequential choices outside that authorization.
